🚀 How to get your free Turso credentials (in 1 minute)

1 Sign up on Turso

Go to turso.tech and log in with your GitHub account (1-click, no credit card required).

2 Create a Database

Click Create Database, give it a name like opsforge-db, and choose any nearby region.

3 Copy URL & Generate Token

Copy the Database URL (libsql://...), then click Generate Token and copy the token string.

4 Paste on Both Devices

Paste the URL and Token here on your laptop and in Termux on your phone. Both devices will now share the same real-time cloud database!
